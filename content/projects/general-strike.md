Flyer and graphics inviting the public to a yearly commemoration honoring the day in 1941 when workers in the Netherlands put on a General Strike in solidarity with victims of the holocaust. This was both poignant and ironic given that the Netherlands was actively complicit in another genocide during this commemoration, and there was no call for a general strike now.
