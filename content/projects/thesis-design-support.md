Half-year mentorship program I designed to support 3 talented Design Academy masters students, and one AKI ArtEZ Bachelors student, with the design and production of their printed thesis through risography. Starting with a private open call application, coordinating with the Masters department heads to reach students, and selected three students from 3 separate departments, plus an additional student from a Crossmedia Bachelors in Enschede. Culminating in 4 thesis risoprinted books, one was part of the thesis project which won the Gijs Bakker Award in 2025.
