Pop-up exhibition set up to sell a curated selection of 18 different risoprinted art prints from international, national, and Eindhoven-based artists and illustrators. Ran an open call to invite artists to submit digital art files for the competition, and selected works were translated into risograph prints by Onomatopee technicians.
