“Does Marxism Support Decolonization? An Indigenous Analysis of a Eurocentric Theory” is an educational book for those who want Indigenous liberation, and seek its articulation in revolutionary discourse.

Published in April 2026, this book was made to educate settlers on the colonial history of the lands they occupy. It combats a settler Marxist tendency to reduce all contradictions of oppression to “class struggle” and reject demands to return stolen land to the original stewards.

This book provides analysis based on primary source material on how Marxism would in fact align with (and could be applied to) Decolonization, while also breaking down the ways that Marxist theory falls short due to its Eurocentricity.

“Does Marxism Support Decolonization?” was written by an author from the Comanche nation who chooses to remain anonymous. This book is edited, designed, and risoprinted by Tolomeo.
