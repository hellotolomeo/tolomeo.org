The Love Letters Hotline & Audio Tour was a project initiated by the Office of Queer Affairs (OoQA) in the Van Abbemuseum in Eindhoven that explores the extent by which a collective, local, queer voice can both literally and figuratively intervene in the museum’s collection presentation.

The project consists of a community-built, semi-conversational, always-in-the-making interactive audio tour that is co-built by members of the Office of Queer Affairs (OoQA) and Eindhoven's local queer community.

12 artworks were selected and then annotated with personal ads inspired by the 90's "On Our Backs"-style lesbian personals. These ads are written from the "POV of the artwork" and were found dispersed in the exhibition space and in the audio tour.

Each personal ad prompted the reader/listener with a question related to their interpersonal experiences of queerness in relation to the artwork. They were invited to respond by calling a phone number and leaving a message. This "Love Letter" was automatically added to a queue, and twice a year, the OoQA team inspected the queue of Love Letters, and chose a selection to publish to the tour.

From 2020 until 2026, the audio tour cumulatively grew and transformed into a conversational piece

I was the main coordinator, and participated in collective ideation, development, and ad-writing.

The tour was translated to Dutch and NGT (Dutch Sign Language).
