A film-and-discussion series, featuring heavily politicized movies on culture, revolution, and the impacts of Eurocentrism onto the world. Revolting Cinema uses movies as a way to spread anticolonial education and foster community engagement and critical conversations in and around the art community in Eindhoven.

Collaborators: Stroomhuis, Olga Mink, Queerhoven, Van Abbehuis.
